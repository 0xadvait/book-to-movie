# The Shepherd's Boy and the Wolf: the film

A one-minute film of one of Aesop's fables, in V. S. Vernon Jones's 1912 translation (public domain). The whole fable, told
straight, drawn as an engraved paper theatre.

| Part | From | The story we tell | Set pieces | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the whole fable | A boy cries wolf for fun, twice. The people stop believing him. Then the wolf comes. | a hill meadow by day, dusk and night; a village street by day, dusk and night | 1 |

The two sets each come back three times under different light, so six scenes need only two places. That is the idea to copy
on a real book: most of the drawing budget is saved by returning to places, not inventing new ones.
